Return the number of days from date 1 (`y1`-`m1`-`d1`) to date 2 (`y2`-`m2`-`d2`): positive if date 2
is later, negative if it is earlier, 0 if the dates are equal.

Use the proleptic Gregorian calendar: a year is a leap year if it is divisible by 4, except years
divisible by 100 that are not divisible by 400. Both dates are valid, with years from 1 to 9999.
